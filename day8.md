# 🚀 Day 8 — 100 Days of Cybersecurity Challenge
Today’s focus was on one of the most important principles in ethical hacking:
👉 Never attack real-world systems. Always practice in legal, controlled environments.

Instead of targeting live applications, ethical hackers build and use legal hacking labs — environments designed with intentional vulnerabilities. These labs simulate real-world attack scenarios while ensuring that learning remains safe, legal, and ethical.

# Through vulnerable labs, we can:

- Understand real attack vectors and exploitation techniques

- Practice identifying and exploiting common security flaws

- Analyze how vulnerabilities are abused in real-world scenarios

- Sharpen both offensive and defensive cybersecurity skills

- Build confidence without crossing legal or ethical boundaries

This approach reflects how professional security researchers and penetration testers are trained — not by breaking laws, but by mastering skills responsibly.

Ethical hacking isn’t just about technical ability; it’s about discipline, responsibility, and respect for the law.
Day 8 reinforced the mindset that skills mean nothing without ethics.
