# 🔐 100 Days of Cybersecurity – Day 9 🔐

Today I explored two powerful privacy tools that protect our digital identity — VPNs and Proxies.

# I learned how these technologies work behind the scenes to:
-   Hide IP addresses
 -  Route traffic securely
-  Control access to online content
-  Improve privacy and anonymity

Understanding the difference between VPN and Proxy made it clear why VPNs provide stronger security with encryption, while proxies act mainly as intermediaries for basic anonymity.
